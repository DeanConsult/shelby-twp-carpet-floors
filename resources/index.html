Floor Care Tips
Tips for Protecting & Maintaining Your Floors
1

Vacuum Before You Mop

For hardwood, vinyl, and laminate, vacuum on a hard floor setting before mopping. Sweeping can scatter fine grit that acts like sandpaper underfoot. Regular vacuuming is the single best thing you can do to protect your floors.

2

Clean Spills Immediately

Any liquid left sitting — even water — can seep into seams, swell planks, or set stains into carpet fibers. Blot (don’t rub) spills right away with a clean cloth. For carpet, use cold water and a mild cleaner.

3

Use Furniture Pads

Heavy furniture dragged across floors leaves permanent scratches in hardwood and gouges in vinyl. Attach felt pads to the legs of all chairs, tables, and sofas. Replace pads when they wear thin — a thin pad is almost worse than no pad.

4

Manage Humidity Levels

Hardwood floors are sensitive to humidity. Keep indoor levels between 35–55% year-round. Too dry and planks crack; too humid and they swell and buckle. A whole-house humidifier or dehumidifier helps maintain the right balance.

5

Use Rugs in High-Traffic Areas

Entry halls, kitchen work zones, and living room paths take the most abuse. Area rugs protect your floors and can be replaced far more cheaply than refinishing hardwood or replacing carpet. Always use a rug pad underneath.

6

Professional Carpet Cleaning

Vacuum carpet at least once a week to lift dirt before it works into the fibers. Have carpet professionally hot-water extracted every 12–18 months to remove deep-set dirt, allergens, and bacteria that vacuuming can’t reach.
